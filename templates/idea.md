## 🎯Problem & solution framing

### Problem space

#### What problem are we trying to solve, and why is it important?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Describe the pain point or unmet need; include evidence from user research.
</details><br>

#### Who is the target customer or persona?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Identify and describe the specific customer segment that this idea is for:

- use your team's persona descriptions,
- the right part of the [value proposition canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas),
- or have a look at chapter 3 of Dan Olsen's *The Lean Product Playbook*.
</details><br>

#### How are customers behaving today?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Current behaviors, workarounds, or friction points; check analytics and interviews.
</details><br>

#### What change in customer behavior would indicate success?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Define the desired outcome as a behavioral change (see Josh Seiden's *Outcomes Over Output* for the definition of outcomes).
</details><br>

### Solution space

#### How might we solve this problem?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Outline the primary solution idea with focus on customer value and intended outcomes. You could write ["how might we" statements](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-might-we-questions/). Avoid prescribing technical architecture unless a specific solution element is explicitly required. Link to design prototypes.
</details><br>

<details>
<summary>Implementation ideas</summary>

Optional implementation options for exploration only.

- These are non-binding ideas, not delivery commitments.
- Final architecture and technical choices are decided by the delivery team.
- Only implementation details explicitly defined outside this block are treated as fixed solution scope.

</details><br>

#### What alternative solutions could we consider before picking one?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

List at least 2–3 options (Torres' continuous discovery principle: compare and contrast).
</details><br>

#### How does this solution deliver value compared to alternatives?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

State the value proposition; use the left part of the [value proposition canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas).
</details><br>

#### How does this solution fit into the bigger product vision or roadmap?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Give some details how this idea relates to the bigger picture, e.g. what would be the next steps. Explain dependencies, future scope, and alignment.
</details><br>

Complete the idea description up to this point before moving to stage **3. Assumptions, risks & validation 🔍**

## 🔍Assumptions, risks & validation

### How does this solution create impact?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Create an impact map on the discovery page to describe how the solution idea connects to desired outcomes.
</details><br>

### What assumptions must be true for this idea to succeed?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Before collecting assumptions, make sure the idea links a discovery page. If there is none, create one from the discovery template and link it first. Keep detailed assumptions on the discovery page and keep this section condensed and numbered (Assumption N: short title).
</details><br>

### Which assumptions are most risky?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Rate assumptions in an assumption map (on the discovery page). Identify the most critical ones to test first.
</details><br>

### How will we test our riskiest assumptions?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

For each critical assumption, define a lean experiment and track it as an item of its own.
</details><br>

### What did we learn from our experiments?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Collect experiment insights and link them back to this idea. Ensure every insight has traceability to the assumption it tested.
</details><br>

### What is our risk assessment?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Record risk scores for this idea and note the discussion and alignment that follows.
</details><br>

### What decision will we make based on this evidence?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Persevere, pivot, or stop.
</details><br>

Move on to stage **4. Delivery & support 🚀** if the risk is sufficiently low, otherwise continue with discovery.

## 🚀 Delivery & support

### How do we deliver this solution effectively while staying true to outcomes?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Work with engineering and design to implement iteratively. Ensure discovery learnings carry into delivery.
</details><br>

### What risks still need to be monitored during delivery?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Track usability gaps, technical feasibility issues, or scope creep.
</details><br>

### What outcomes and metrics will define success post-launch?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Align on leading indicators (customer behavior) and lagging indicators (business impact).
</details><br>

### How will we validate value during delivery?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Plan usability tests, feature flags, or early-access releases.
</details><br>

When this solution is built and delivered to users, move on to stage **5. Post-launch learning 📊**

## 📊 Post-launch learning

### Did the solution deliver the intended outcomes?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Track customer behavior and business metrics against success criteria.
</details><br>

### What customer feedback and insights did we gather?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Capture qualitative and quantitative input.
</details><br>

### What should we do next?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Decide whether to scale, iterate, or retire the solution. Feed learnings back into discovery.
</details><br>
