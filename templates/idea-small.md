</details><br>

#### Who is the target customer or persona?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Who experiences this issue or benefits from the change?<br>Use your team's persona descriptions.
</details><br>

#### How are customers behaving today?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Short note on current workaround or impact if unsolved.
</details><br>

### Solution

#### How might we solve this problem?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Describe the idea briefly with focus on value and intended outcome; link to a mock-up or a ticket if available. Avoid prescribing technical implementation unless a specific solution element must be fixed.
</details><br>

<details>
<summary>Implementation ideas</summary>

Optional implementation options for exploration only.

- These are non-binding ideas, not delivery commitments.
- Final architecture and technical choices are decided by the delivery team.
- Only implementation details explicitly defined outside this block are treated as fixed solution scope.
</details><br>

#### What value does this solution create?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

Summarize the main benefit (time saved, clarity, usability, etc.).
</details><br>

#### How does this solution fit into the bigger product vision or roadmap?

<details>
<summary>Hints for this question</summary>

One sentence on how it supports the product vision or another initiative.
</details><br>

Move on to stage **4 Delivery & support 🚀** if the risk is sufficiently low, otherwise continue with discovery.
