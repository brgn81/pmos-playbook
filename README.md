# pm-os-playbook

The PMOS Playbook is the PM layer of [PMOS](https://github.com/brgn81/pm-os): skills, agents, rules and prompts for product management work, plus the PMOS types they work on. [PMOS](https://github.com/brgn81/pm-os-footing) installs it into a product workspace at a pinned ref.

Status: setup. Content moves here from `brgn81/pm-os-legacy` with issue P1.

Scope, architecture, decisions and roadmap live in [brgn81/pm-os](https://github.com/brgn81/pm-os/tree/main/docs). Instructions for working in this repo: [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).
